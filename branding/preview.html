MuxCore — Brand Mark Concepts

Round 3: F and K were the strongest marks so far — pure thread geometry, no frame or literal object, genuine two-tone crossings — so everything else was dropped. Both got a small refinement (terminal hubs on F; a real overlapping crossing plus a "core" bead on K, instead of just two petals touching at a point), and three new concepts (N, O, P) extend the same closed-loop, two-tone, real-math-crossing language in new directions: a braid bent into a ring, a three-petal rose, and two literally interlocked rings. All generated from real geometry — see generate_logos.py. Same palette throughout: teal #3DB8A8 on near-black #0B0C0F, amber #E6A23C as the accent. Each card also shows the mark at favicon size (32px).
